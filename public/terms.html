We aim to match the response to the conduct — a one-off mistake is treated differently than a deliberate or repeated one. The steps above are our usual path, not a guarantee: where practical we'll give notice and a chance to respond, but where the conduct is serious we may move straight to suspension or permanent removal without a prior warning (see below).
