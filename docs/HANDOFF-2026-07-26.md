## The wave: nutrition week-block programs (#1834)

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-25-nutrition-week-block-programs.md`.
Read it before writing code — it is the build contract, not background.

### The bug it exists to remove

The nutritionist `program` build type emits a week-phase outline
(`Week 1 — Reset & habits`, …). `BSProAssignPage`'s `isNutri` branch maps every
block through `bsAssignMeal`, so assigning it installs a **7-day menu where
every day carries meals named "Reset & habits", "Build routine", "Dial macros",
"Lock it in"** — fabricated food on the client's Eat tab.

### The blocker that reorders the work

C's premise is a different menu each week. **The builder cannot author a
different menu each DAY.** `iosAppBroadsheetPros.jsx:3362` assigns the *same*
`meals` array to all seven days, and the `meal_plan` builder emits one day's
list. A client on an assigned meal plan today eats the identical five meals
every day of the week. Stack four weekly rows on that and you get four weeks of
the same five meals.

**Per-day variation is the prerequisite and is worth more on its own than
per-week.**

### Owner rulings — binding, do not re-litigate

1. **Authoring: week 1, then vary it — and the AI drafts week 1.** Later weeks
   start as copies the coach edits. An unedited copy is a week the coach *chose*
   to repeat, never food the app invented.
2. **A purchased program carries a TERM; access ends.** A repeatable sale, not a
   one-time one.
3. **The term starts on START, not purchase.** A never-started purchase has no
   clock running.
4. **After the last week the final week persists — while entitled.** Monthly
   coach plan: it keeps serving until the coach assigns the next thing. One-off
   program: the term ends, the menu stops, the re-buy is offered.
5. **On a menu conflict the COACH chooses at assign time** — replace the standing
   menu, or pause it for the term and restore it after.
6. **All of it applies to TRAINING too.** A purchased program carries a term in
   both disciplines; a single session is consumed by attendance, not by time.

Unruled because it is a correctness rule, not a product call: **a plain
Meal-plan assignment must keep behaving exactly as it does now.**

### Three findings the rulings surfaced

- **① The AI is already called and its answer is thrown away.**
  `iosAppBroadsheetPros.jsx:6052` (nutritionist) and `:5171` (trainer) both
  `await window.ShapeAI?.generatePlanDraft?.(…)`, render "Generating…" and an
  **✦ AI DRAFT** eyebrow, then build the outline from a hardcoded template
  (`:6055`). Coaches get a template; the call costs money to produce nothing.
  Ruling 1 is mostly *finishing* this, not adding it.
- **② A purchased program is recorded as a "booking."**
  `src/app/api/stripe/checkout-session/route.ts:232` derives `kind` from the
  provider's role and nothing else, so a 12-week program and a single Tuesday
  session are the same row shape, separable only by `plan_id`.
- **③ Training programs are already replayable forever.**
  `ShapeSelfTraining.startPurchasedPlan` re-runs a plan under a fresh `runId`
  with no bound. Live today.

### Build order

**C0 → (C1 ∥ E) → C2 → C3.** C0 is independent and ships first.

**Storage shape today:** `client_meal_plans` holds ONE week (`payload.days` is a
7-entry day-of-week menu); exactly one published row per (nutritionist, client) —
`POST /api/nutritionist/meal-plan` **archives every published row for the pair**
before inserting; `/api/client/plan` selects `published` / `created_at desc` /
`limit 1`, and `week_start` is stored but never used to select.

⚠ **The hazard:** `merge_program_detail(p_client_id, p_patch)` merges **top-level**
keys, so patching `{ nutrition: {...} }` replaces the whole nutrition object and
silently wipes the coach's calorie/macro targets. Use the discipline-scoped
`set_program_detail(client, discipline, phase, detail)` instead.

⚠ **Week-time semantics are defined ONCE in the spec** and reused by
`phaseStartISO`, C2's `week_start`, and E's term: member-local `YYYY-MM-DD`
dates, Monday-based (`bsRepeatSpec`'s `0 = MONDAY` — the reminders table's
`0 = Sunday` is a *different* base), boundaries owned by `shape_user_tz(uid)`.
The coach-clock version of this bug already shipped once and needed
`2026-07-20-cycle-coach-today.sql` to fix. Do not re-make it.
