## 5. ⚠ Two environment facts that cost this session hours

and every call after the first starts a FRESH sandbox.** Six full pipeline runs were lost
to reclamation, two of them *after* the render completed and before upload. What works:
**one background script that does everything** (boot → layers → render → upload) with
the upload as the very next line after the render, and hosts tried in the recipe's order
(uguu → litterbox → gofile). What does not: anything split across calls, and any run that
includes the ~5-minute app capture *and* a render.

CONNECT). Media can be fetched, rendered and md5'd only in the sandbox; the owner opens
the links, not the agent. `npm i ffmpeg-static` gives a local ffmpeg 7.0.2, but nothing
