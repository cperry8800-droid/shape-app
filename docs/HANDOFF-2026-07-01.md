## Architecture you'll need
- **Verify SECDEF grants** after any RPC change: `has_function_privilege('anon'|'authenticated', '<fn>(<args>)'::regprocedure, 'EXECUTE')`.
- **The revoke gotcha** (WORKLOG #1459): a bare `revoke … from public` leaves the `anon`/`authenticated` grants — always `revoke … from public, anon, authenticated` and re-`grant … to service_role`.
- **RLS write-scope pattern** (`2026-06-17-coach-write-scope.sql`, reused by #1472/#1473): split a broad `FOR ALL` policy into owner-scoped SELECT/UPDATE/DELETE + a stricter INSERT gated on `is_discipline_coach_on_client`.
- **Store pricing is now DB-authoritative** — keep `store_catalogue` in sync with `src/lib/store-catalogue.ts`.
