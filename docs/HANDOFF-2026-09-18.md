## Implementation

- `public/newdesign/workoutDocument.js` is the shared canonical workout normalizer and assignment converter. Both coach editors preserve its structured weeks/days/exercises, instructions, media, legacy outlines, and unknown metadata.
- Website `dashBuilder.jsx` and mobile `BSWorkoutDocumentEditor.jsx` provide single-day/program editing, multi-add, duplication, scheduling, draft recovery, video upload/reuse, explicit publishing, and revision-aware saves through `/api/coach/plans`.
- `/api/coach/plans/assignments` previews eligible future updates. The web/mobile apply flows carry the original snapshots through the shared publisher, reject stale selections, retain dates and program instructions, and clear removed optional prescription fields.
- Client Quick Log and `workoutSession.mjs` support direct workout launch, prefilled sets, automatic rest/advancement/supersets, account-bound recovery, frozen prescriptions, honest unknown timing, and atomic session saves.
- Coach dashboard changes distinguish unknown/unavailable progress from zero progress, improve the roster/attention view, and provide accessible client details and durable private notes.
