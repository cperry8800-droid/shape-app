## What the owner asked, and what shipped

1. **"these lights are too thick blocking, screen."** The stage and club moving-head beams read as fat
   columns with a beaded ripple over the LED screen. Both beam shaders now draw a thin bright core
   with a faint wide glow. The beams are narrower (half-angle 0.009 rad on the stage, 0.007 in the
   club). The brightness ripple along each beam is gone; bloom turned it into beads. The beams also
   fade out near the lens, so a shot looking up a beam does not fill the frame.
2. **"remove this walk way in front of stage."** The runway and the B-stage are removed, and so is the
   crowd's exclusion lane for them. The floor now fills to the stage lip: the crowd is 1,397 on high
   (1,378 before).
3. **"have it say club shape with the logo triangles above it, remove the outside 2 large
   triangles."** The screen shows the Shape mark's teal ▸ and white ◂ above **CLUB SHAPE**, in the
   wordmark's own face. The two 6.2 m statues are removed.
   - The brand art only has the letters S H A P E. So `wordmarkGlyphs.mjs` builds each letter from
     slanted strokes, fitted to `public/SHAPE-logo-teal-white.png`, and C L U B reuse the same
     strokes. The rebuilt S H A P E each match the PNG at IoU ≥ 0.9, and a test holds them to it.
   - The cap height is snapped to exactly 12 LED dots, so every horizontal bar lands on one row of
     dots and reads crisp rather than as two dim rows.
   - `node screen-mask.mjs` regenerates `src/clubShapeMask.mjs`. A test fails if the committed mask is
     out of date. `logo-mask.cjs` and `shapeLogoMask.mjs` are deleted.
4. **"i want this to be as cinematic as possible."** `src/cinematic.mjs` adds a post chain that runs
   on the high tier (desktop) by default. The low tier and phones are unchanged. The passes, in
   order:
   - **Scene** renders into its own multisampled target with a depth texture.
   - **Light shafts**: the LED wall's bright pixels, found by rebuilding each pixel's world position
     from depth, are smeared toward the wall's sun at quarter resolution. Nora, the rig and heads cut
     dark bars through them. They swell with the music level and the drop.
   - **Depth of field** is set per shot. Hands, face and screen shots are shallow; the room shots are
     deep. The two shots over the crowd focus on the crowd. A cut changes the lens at once, like a new
     camera; within a shot, focus eases like a focus puller.
   - **Anamorphic streaks** stretch the brightest lights sideways in a cool blue-white.
   - **Film finish**: split-tone grade, gentle S-curve, lens fringing toward the corners, vignette,
     fine grain (held still under reduced motion), and 2.39:1 bars on landscape frames.
   - `?cine=0` turns the whole chain off; `?bars=0` keeps it without the bars.
   - Nothing in it flashes, and it uses no pictures.
5. **"actually remove the stairs on the sides of the stage."** The four LED-faced steps that rose to
   the wall on each wing are gone, with their nosing lines and the teal riser chase in the wall
   shader. The wings are a flat deck from the booth's sides to the balconies, and the wall stands on
   it. A test holds the wings and their par cans at or below the deck, and the wall as the only LED
   surface; putting one step back fails it (checked, with and without an LED face). Rendered in the
   panorama and crane shots: the wall meets the deck, nothing else moved.

  full-screen passes, most at quarter resolution.
- **Cost, unmeasured on a GPU.** In SwiftShader the cinematic chain doubles the frame time (3.7 s to
  7.3 s a frame). That is a CPU rasteriser and says little about a GPU. The expensive pass is the
  depth of field: 28 taps at full resolution, each reading the scene, the shafts and depth. On a
  retina laptop (pixel ratio 2) that is the thing to watch.
- **Download:** `pub/booth.js` is 1.51 MB, the same as before. The screen mask is about 21 KB, where
  the old logo mask was about 26 KB.
- **Phone layout (390 px, low tier, served wrapped the way the artifact host wraps it):** no
  horizontal overflow, the set starts and plays, no page errors.

## Lessons worth keeping

- **Bloom turns a brightness ripple into beads.** A slow sine along a beam was invisible on its own and
  read as a string of pearls once bloom thresholded it.
- **Snap lettering to the dot grid.** A cap height that is a whole number of dots puts each bar on one
  row. A fractional one spreads it over two dim rows and the word reads soft.
- **Ease the lens within a shot, never across a cut.** At a low frame rate, easing across a cut left the
  face shot blurred for its first seconds, because the lens was still set for the wide shot.
- **A shot aimed at the floor needs its own focus distance.** Behind Nora and over her shoulder, the
  aim point is on the floor 14 m out, so focusing on it blurred the crowd that fills the frame.
- **`pub/index.html` is a fragment.** The artifact host adds the viewport meta. Served raw, a phone
  lays it out at 980 px, so a local phone check must load a wrapped copy (the README says how).
- **Run the measurement panel alone** (unchanged): renders beside it make SwiftShader time out.
