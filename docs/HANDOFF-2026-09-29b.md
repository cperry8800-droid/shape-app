- **Lasers:**
  - 12 emitters on the portal (6 on low), drawn as camera-facing additive ribbons.
  - Each ribbon ends where its ray leaves the room box.
  - Three patterns rotate every 8 bars (fan, tunnel, balcony sweep); the fan runs on the drop.
- **Moving heads** change colour by section.
- **Blinders:**
  - 6 units on the stage lip (4 on low).
  - They flash with the kick only on the drop, **at most once a beat**, so under 3 flashes a second.
- **Reduced motion:** blinders drop to a steady 35% glow and the laser sweeps slow down.

### Two new camera shots

These are `src/noraDirector.mjs`. They appear in the camera row and in Auto.

- **Drone (40 bars):**
  - It skims the crowd from behind the booth.
  - It climbs to the left balconies, runs along the boxes and rises to the ceiling.
  - It looks back down the whole room, crosses to the right balconies, drops to the far end and
    skims back over the crowd to the stage.
  - The path is a closed Catmull-Rom spline with an arc-length table, so the speed is even and the
    loop is seamless.

## Lessons worth keeping

- **A rim light that crosses behind the performer lands on the opposite deck.**
  - What happened: the amber right rim turned the left deck bronze. Swapping the environment map
    did not change it. Turning lights off one at a time (`lightprobe.cjs`) found the cause in one
    pass.
  - The fix: rims aimed level at head height (target y 1.68) in a 0.15-radian cone.
- **QEM decimation can leave zero-length normals.**
  - The symptom: they make NaN lighting, which showed up as white streaks through the far crowd.
    Hiding the hair was what traced it.
  - The fix: `geometryFor` rebuilds any normal shorter than 1/64 as an outward direction from the
    body axis at load time.
  - Hair also gets a rim tinted by the hair colour, so it no longer glows ice-blue.
- **A camera path through a crowd needs clearance checks.** The first drone start sat inside the
  raised hands, and the return leg clipped palm fronds. Both were moved.
  `_droneForTest` exposes the path for checking.
