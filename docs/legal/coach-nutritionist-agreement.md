This Coach / Nutritionist Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is between the operating entity
above ("**Shape**," "we," "us") and the trainer or nutritionist who accepts it (the
"**Coach**," "you"). It governs your use of Shape to offer and deliver coaching services.
It is in addition to Shape's consumer **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**, which
also apply to you. If this Agreement conflicts with the consumer Terms on a coach-specific
matter, this Agreement controls.

---

## 1. Independent contractor status
You are an **independent contractor**, not an employee, partner, joint venturer, or agent
of Shape. Nothing here creates an employment or agency relationship. You control how you
deliver your services — your methods, schedule, programming, pricing, and brand. You are
responsible for your own business expenses, equipment, and benefits. You may not bind
Shape to any obligation or represent that you speak for Shape.

## 2. Eligibility, credentials & scope of practice
- You represent and warrant that you hold the licenses and certifications you list on your
  profile (e.g., NASM, ACE, ISSA, CSCS, NSCA, RD, RDN, CNS, or state license), that they
  are current and in good standing, and that you will keep them current while active on
  Shape.
- You will practice **only within the scope** your credential and local law permit.
  Nutritionists providing medical nutrition therapy or any service requiring a license
  must hold the appropriate state/national license.
- Shape may **request verification** of your credentials and insurance at any time, and
  may display a "**Verified**" badge only where verification has been completed. Until
  then, your credentials are presented as **self-reported**.
- You will not give medical diagnosis or treatment, and you will refer clients to a
  qualified medical professional where appropriate.

## 3. Services & the coach–client relationship
- You are **solely responsible** for the services you deliver — programming, advice,
  communication, scheduling, and timeliness.
- The coaching contract is **between you and your client**. Shape is the marketplace and
  software provider; Shape is **not a party** to that contract and does not supervise,
  direct, or guarantee your services.
- You retain full ownership of your brand, methodology, programs, and client
  relationships.

## 4. Payments, platform fee & payouts
- You **set your own rates** for subscriptions, sessions, programs, and meal plans.
- Shape charges a **15% platform fee** on gross revenue you receive through the platform.
  Standard Stripe card-processing fees are deducted separately from the net amount.
- Payments are processed and paid out through **Stripe Connect** to the connected account
  you set up; payouts follow the schedule you configure with Stripe and Stripe's terms.
- Shape may offset against your payouts any amounts you owe Shape or any refunds/chargebacks
  owed to clients.
- **Refunds/chargebacks:** [describe how refunds and disputed charges are handled and who
  bears them — confirm with counsel and align with the consumer ToS refund language].

## 5. Taxes
You are responsible for all taxes on income you earn through Shape. Shape (via Stripe)
issues **1099-K** or similar tax documents where required by law. You are responsible for
your own tax filings, withholdings, and any sales/VAT obligations.

## 6. Content & intellectual property
- You **retain ownership** of the content you create and upload (programs, meal plans,
  media, bios, messages).
- You grant Shape a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to host, display,
  transmit, and (for previews/marketplace listings) reproduce that content **solely to
  operate, promote, and improve the service**. This license ends when you delete the
  content or close your account, except to the extent clients have already received it
  (e.g., a program a client purchased or downloaded).
- The Shape name, logo, and platform look-and-feel remain Shape's property.
- **Music/audio:** if you attach playlists or "soundtracks," they play through the
  client's own connected music service under that service's license. You are responsible
  for ensuring any playlists you create and share comply with the relevant provider's
  terms. Do not upload audio you don't have the rights to.

## 7. Client data, confidentiality & privacy
- When a client links with you, you receive the client information needed to coach them —
  goals, training/nutrition data, and the client's health-and-readiness screening
  (injuries, conditions, medications, emergency contact).
- You must keep client information **confidential**, use it **only** to provide your
  services, retain it no longer than needed, and **not** sell, publish, or repurpose it.
- You will comply with all privacy and data-protection laws applicable to your practice,
  and with Shape's Privacy Policy and Data & Compliance practices.
- Access ends when the client cancels the relationship. Misuse of client data is grounds
  for immediate removal.

## 8. Professional conduct & compliance
You will: act lawfully and professionally; not harass, discriminate, or endanger clients;
follow your credential's standards of practice; not make false or misleading claims
(including about results or your qualifications); and carry out your services with
reasonable skill and care.

## 10. Non-circumvention
You may not encourage or arrange for clients you reach through Shape to move their payments
**off-platform** to avoid the platform fee while still using Shape's tools to deliver the
service.

## 11. Term & termination
- Either party may end this Agreement at any time. You may close your coach account from
  settings; Shape may suspend or remove your profile for credential fraud, scope-of-
  practice violations, repeated complaints, prohibited conduct, non-payment of amounts
  owed, or material breach.
- On termination: active client subscriptions handle per the consumer ToS; **earned but
  unpaid payouts** are processed on the normal schedule, minus any amounts owed back to
  Shape or to clients as refunds; and the sections that by their nature survive (payment
  obligations, IP license to delivered content, confidentiality, disclaimers, liability,
  indemnity, dispute resolution) continue.

## 12. Disclaimers, liability & indemnification
- Shape provides the marketplace and software "as is." Shape does not guarantee any volume
  of clients, earnings, or results.
- To the fullest extent permitted by law, Shape is not liable for indirect, incidental,
  consequential, special, or punitive damages, and Shape's total liability is limited as
  set out in the consumer Terms.
- You agree to **defend, indemnify, and hold Shape harmless** from claims arising out of
  your services, your content, your violation of law or any third-party right, your
  professional conduct, or your breach of this Agreement.

## 13. Dispute resolution
Governing law, informal-resolution-first, binding individual arbitration, and class-action
waiver as set out in the consumer **Terms of Service** apply to this Agreement, except as
required otherwise by law.

## 14. Changes & contact
Shape may update this Agreement; material changes will be communicated in advance, and
continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance. Questions:
info@theshapecommunity.com.
