Commits `411ba3d` (items 1–5, a checkpoint) and the one after it (items 6–7 and these records).

1. **Regrade and relight.** Lounges warm and muted; upholstery, tables and fascias darkened toward
   the reference; lasers mostly white and ice, with teal only on the drop; the crowd lit by the stage
   (a rim and fill that swell with the level and lift on the kick).
2. **Balcony fronts.** Frameless glass on a dark stone kerb, a slim handrail with a warm LED line,

3. **The floor crowd.** Desktop high gains a third level of detail (`tiny`) and goes from 697 to

   clothes, mostly dark hair.
4. **Real 3D palms** (`src/palmGeometry.mjs`): ringed trunks and folded leaflets, no leaf cards, uplit
   gold by the bake.
5. **The light bake** (`src/lightBake.mjs`, pure, tested). At load: direct light (soft-core
   inverse-square, cones, shadow rays through a box grid), hemisphere AO and one bounce, written into
   the venue's vertex colours and the floor canvas. The painted spill, glow discs and candle halos
   are gone. **It downloads nothing:** the cost is load time (table below).
6. **The arena stage** (`src/arenaStage.mjs`, tested):
   - One LED wall across the whole stage end, **floor to ceiling** (≈20.6 × 15.3 m). To make room, the
     top-tier bridge that crossed the stage end is removed (its slab, rail, soffit line and two palms);
     every tier's side balcony now runs on to the end wall.
   - Its content is a shader, no images: a dawn over a dark sea. A sun rises just over the booth, rays
     fan from it, ridges stand either side, ribbons of teal and ice drift high up, and the sun's
     reflection breaks into dashes below. A slow teal chase runs along the step risers.
   - Tiered wings with LED risers, a runway and a round B-stage into the crowd, par cans on the lip.
   - The mark as two statues: the logo's ▸ ◂ triangles, 6.2 m tall, uplit warm gold at their feet and
     cooling to ice at the top, the ◂ raised as in the logo.
   - A truss rig at y 13 (front, middle, back and sides): 32 spots, 17 moving heads (6 on low) throwing
     narrow beams in three looks that change every 8 bars, and two line arrays.
   - The drone's flight stays at least a metre from the rig (a test; the closest pass is 1.5 m).
7. **One large screen.** The black portal monolith that framed a separate 7.2 × 4.2 m screen is gone.
   `club.mjs` now draws its Club Shape artwork (the square, the mark, CLUB SHAPE and the spectrum)
   **additively onto the arena wall, on that wall's own 75 mm dot grid** (14.4 × 8.4 m, edges on the
   grid), so the whole stage end is one screen.
   - Nora's back light (a rect light the size and place of the old screen) is kept as it was, so her

   - The top truss over the booth (its moving heads and her hair light) now hangs on two chain hoists

     lasers are placed by it.

## Lessons worth keeping

- **A mirrored triangle winds the other way.** The ◂ statue's face was back-face culled (only its white
  edges showed) because it is the ▸ mirrored. Its material is double-sided now.
- **The arena wall's content has to work around what stands in front of it.** The booth, the statues
  and the steps cover most of the lower wall, so the sun, the horizon and the ribbons sit where the wall
  is actually seen: above the booth and across the top.
- **An additive overlay only reads as the same screen if its dots coincide.** The Club Shape artwork's
  panel edges sit on the wall's 75 mm grid, it adds nothing where it is dark, and fog only dims it; any
  of those wrong and a rectangle shows.
- **Run the measurement panel alone.** With renders running beside it, `shot3` and `phone-run` time out
  on page load in SwiftShader.
- **`crop.cjs` needs absolute paths** (it writes a temporary page next to the output and opens it by
  `file://`).
