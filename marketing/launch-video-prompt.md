# SHAPE — Social Launch Video

Higgsfield shot-by-shot prompt pack · ~53 s · 9:16 + 16:9

---

## 1 · The concept

**"THE DAY"** — one day, dawn to dark, told as a single continuous fall through a
nocturnal world.

Not a fitness ad: no gym, no sweat, no before/after, no one smiling at camera. The product
appears as **physical light in the room** — Shape's Broadsheet typography floating as glass
plates in real space — never as a phone held up to the lens.

**The spine:** `05:58 train → 07:14 eat → your coach → the lock → 22:40 recover → your
score → the world.`

The engine of the whole film is **Heart-Rate Sync**. Shape Radio matches the station's BPM
to yours, and the film's own soundtrack obeys that rule for all 53 seconds. `STATION 58 /
YOU 58` at dawn. The tempo climbs as the day climbs. At **0:33** the readout hits `STATION
140 / YOU 140 — IN SYNC` and the track drops at 140. It decays back to 58 for recovery. On
the globe, every pin in the world blooms on that same pulse.

> The music isn't a bed under the product. **The music is the product.** Nobody else can
> make this film.

## 3 · Master style block

Paste this **verbatim** at the head of every single shot prompt. Consistency across
generations depends on it.

> Cinematic 3D-animated short film, photoreal-stylized hybrid. Near-black world of
> paper-grain blacks (#0a0a0a to #141414), volumetric haze, single-source practical
> lighting. The ONLY saturated colour anywhere in frame is a single electric accent —
> everything else is desaturated steel, bone white, and warm tungsten. Anamorphic 2.39:1
> feel, shallow depth of field, fine 35mm grain, no lens flares, no colour fringing. Camera
> moves are slow, deliberate and continuous — one unbroken move per shot, never handheld,
> never whip-pan. Human figures are stylised-realistic, lit softly, usually turned away or
> in silhouette; they are never performing for camera and never make eye contact with it.
> Editorial newspaper typography exists as physical light inside the world: thin sans and
> serif set into floating glass plates with hairline rules and small monospace labels,
> casting real light on nearby surfaces. Mood: calm, nocturnal, expensive, hypnotic.

### Negative prompt — every generation

> gym, barbell, dumbbell, weight rack, treadmill, mirror selfie, flexing, six-pack abs,
> sweat, shouting, motivational poster, teal-and-orange grade, lens flare, anamorphic
> streaks, garbled text, watermark, stock-footage smile to camera, drone city flyover,
> crypto neon grid, HUD sci-fi interface, hexagons, particles, confetti

## 4 · Shot prompts

### Shot 1 — COLD OPEN · 0:00–0:05
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — Extreme macro on a single glowing em-dash cursor blinking in absolute
darkness. Slow dolly back reveals it resting on a vast blank sheet of near-black paper with
visible fibre grain, raked by one hard light from frame left. Dust motes drift through the
beam. The frame is ninety-five percent empty. The camera retreats steadily and the sheet
extends away to a horizon, becoming landscape. Weightless, silent, enormous.

### Shot 2 — THE PAGE TURN · 0:05–0:08
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — The vast black paper sheet peels and turns like a broadsheet newspaper
page, its edge catching a thin electric rim-light as it sweeps across the entire frame.
Behind it lies a second sheet, and through that sheet warm light bleeds in the shape of a
window. The camera pushes forward through the turning page and into the light.

### Shot 3 — 05:58 · TRAIN · 0:08–0:14
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — Interior bedroom before dawn, near-total darkness. A single figure sits
on the edge of a bed in silhouette, seen from behind, lacing one shoe. One cold blue
window, one small warm lamp. Floating in the air beside them hangs a thin glass plate of
editorial typography glowing faintly, anchored in the room like a solid object. On the
figure's wrist a small dark watch face shows one slow pulsing ring. The camera glides
laterally past them at walking pace while the plate holds steady in frame. Breath faintly
visible in the cold. No exercise equipment anywhere.

### Shot 4 — 07:14 · EAT · 0:14–0:21
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — Interior kitchen, early morning, low warm light crossed by one shaft of
cold daylight. Close on a pair of hands working over a pan, steam rising and curling
through the beam. The camera cranes slowly up and forward over the counter. Suspended above
the counter are floating glass typographic plates: a recipe timeline with progress ticks
filling left to right, and beside it a list assembling itself line by line in mid-air,
items sliding into grouped clusters. Photoreal food, real domestic mess, nothing styled for
a magazine.

### Shot 5 — THE SWAP · 0:21–0:25
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — Macro on a single floating glass card hanging in kitchen air. One line of
typography slides out to the left and a different line slides in to replace it, a clean
mechanical exchange, a bright underline drawing itself left to right beneath the new line.
Far behind, deeply out of focus, a shoulder moves through frame. The camera is locked off
and perfectly still — the only motion in the entire frame is the type.

### Shot 6 — THE COACH · 0:25–0:29
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — Hard cut to a different place and a different hour. A small room
somewhere else in the world, blue evening light, rain running down a window. A second
person sits in three-quarter silhouette, reads something, and begins to type. Their words
leave the frame as a single thin line of glowing typography that travels right, out through
the window glass and away into the dark. The camera pushes slowly in on the window as the
line disappears.

### Shot 7 — THE CLIMB · 0:29–0:33
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — Tight on a wrist in motion in low light, a dark watch face catching light
in flashes. The pulsing ring on the watch face beats faster and faster and the number on it
climbs. Behind the wrist, a body moves through darkness in soft focus — running, stairs,
unclear and unglamorous, never a gym. The camera tracks alongside the wrist, accelerating
with it. Tension building, everything tightening toward a break.

### Shot 8 — THE LOCK / THE DROP · 0:33–0:38 ★
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — Extreme close-up, perfectly symmetrical split composition in pure black.
On the left, a watch face pulsing a ring of light. On the right, floating in the dark air, a
glass plate pulsing a matching ring. The two pulses drift visibly out of phase, then slide
toward each other, then snap into perfect sync and beat as one on the same frame. Between
them a single horizontal slider travels to centre and locks. At the instant of lock, a
shockwave of light travels outward through the darkness in every direction. The camera
punches in hard and fast on the moment of impact. Weight and force, not sparkle.

> ★ **This is the money shot** and it's the one place the real app screen goes in full.
> Composite the live HEART-RATE SYNC panel over the generated plate — `STATION 140` on the
> left, `YOU 140` on the right, the slider running to centre, `IN SYNC` resolving beneath it
> and again in the corner. Let the real UI's own accent colour carry this moment; it should
> be the brightest frame in the film. **Hold the completed panel for a full beat before
> cutting.**

### Shot 9 — 22:40 · RECOVER · 0:38–0:42
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — Night. Extreme close on a wrist lying still on dark bedding, the watch
band loose. The pulsing ring slows, and slows, and settles into a long calm rhythm. A single
bright line draws itself across the darkness above the wrist and flattens out. Faint
horizontal hairline rules resolve beneath it like stacked bars. Everything else is pure
black. The camera drifts upward almost imperceptibly.

### Shot 10 — THE SCORE · 0:42–0:46
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — In a black void, all the floating typographic glass plates from the day
rush inward from every edge of frame and stack into one single card, landing with real
weight and a low settle. A circular ring around the card ignites and sweeps once around its
circumference through a cascade of colour — steel, then gold, then teal, then violet, then
rose. The camera punches in on the ring at the exact moment the sweep completes. Impact and
finality.

### Shot 11 — THE GLOBE · 0:46–0:51
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — A dark globe rotating slowly in black space, continents rendered as fine
wireframe with lit coastlines and a soft atmospheric rim-light on the limb. Points of light
bloom on the surface one after another in rhythm — Boston, London, Lagos, São Paulo, Seoul,
Sydney — each bloom a soft expanding ring like a download completing. Thin luminous arcs
draw between pairs of points and remain lit, building a web across the sphere. Subtle
volumetric glow, slow constant rotation, weightless. No country borders, no labels, no
interface elements.

> Seed this with your reference clip as an image/video input in Higgsfield. **One thing must
> change from the reference: pins bloom on the kick drum, not at random.** The whole world
> is on one heartbeat.

### Shot 12 — END CARD · 0:51–0:53
`[STYLE BLOCK]` — The rotating globe contracts smoothly and evenly into a single small solid
mark at the centre of frame, then holds perfectly still in black. Nothing else in the frame
moves. Absolute stillness.

## 7 · Music brief

Dark deep house, minor key, no vocals, no build-and-collapse EDM structure. **The track's
tempo is not fixed — it tracks the heart rate on screen, all the way through.** This is the
brief's one unusual demand and the entire reason the film works.

| Time | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:08 | sub-bass only, pulsing at 58. Felt, not heard. No kick. |
| 0:08 | kick enters on the cut to the bedroom, still 58, deep half-time. |
| 0:14 | closed hats and a rolling bassline, tempo drifting up to ~96. |
| 0:25 | warm pad opens under the coach shot, ~110. |
| 0:29–0:33 | riser, high-pass filter opening, tempo ramping audibly 118 → 132, no kick. |
| 0:33 | **drop at 140.** Full kick on the exact frame the two rings snap into sync. At 140 the track sits closer to dark melodic techno than house — let it. That's the peak of the day. |
| 0:38 | filter closes, tempo decays back down 140 → 58 across four seconds. |
| 0:42 | kick returns full at 140 for the score and the globe. |
| 0:51 | hard cut to silence on the wordmark. Let the last frame sit in nothing. |

> If only one thing in the mix is right, make it this: **the watch ring and the kick drum
> land on the same frame at 0:33.**

## 8 · Four more ideas worth stealing

1. **The day prints itself.** Your design language is a broadsheet — lean all the way in.
   Open on a press: the day's edition setting itself in type, letter by letter, before dawn.
   Nobody else in fitness can do this, because nobody else built their app to look like a
   newspaper. It's the most ownable visual idea you have.
2. **Two people in every frame.** Fitness apps are lonely and everyone knows it. Rule: every
   shot implies a second person — a reply arriving, a swapped meal, a plan someone wrote for
   you. Never state it. Let people notice they're never alone in the film.
3. **Timestamps as the whole script.** `05:58 · 07:14 · 22:40`. No adjectives, no promises,
   no "transform your life." Just the clock. The most confident thing you could put on
   screen, and exactly the Linear/Whoop register.
4. **Cut the last frame's music dead.** Everyone fades out. Silence on the wordmark, after
   50 seconds of a pulse locked to a heartbeat, will make people rewatch it.

## 9 · Production notes (measured, not assumed)

Added while building the end card. Everything here was verified rather than taken on trust.

### The wordmark

- **Use `mobile-app/public/shape-wordmark.png`.** 3626×882, PNG colour type 6 (RGBA), and
  the alpha is genuinely used: **94.0% fully transparent, 5.2% solid ink, 0.8% antialiased
  edge.** No keying needed.
- ⚠ **`mobile-app/SHAPE-wordmark-white.png` IS NOT A FALLBACK — it is byte-identical**
  (md5 `8d2b5e35d6e76d7add21a33ade8c83b1`). Two paths, one file.
- ⚠ `mobile-app/public-runtime/assets/shape-logo-new-white.png` is **187×100** — it has
  alpha, but it would upscale ~3× to hit a 594 px target. Not usable for the end card.
- ⚠ **THE CANVAS IS MOSTLY PADDING, WHICH BREAKS "55% OF FRAME" IF YOU SCALE IT NAIVELY.**
  The ink bounding box is **x 270–3351, y 270–611 → 3082×342**, i.e. 270 px of dead margin
  on every side. Scaling the *canvas* to 55% of 1080 (594 px) renders the visible wordmark
  at 594 × 3082/3626 = **505 px, only 46.8% of frame.** Crop to the ink bbox first, then
  scale, so 55% means 55%.

### The clips

- `minimax_h3` output carries **its own generated audio stream**. Strip it with `-an`, or a
  mux without an explicit `-map 0:v` will silently ship the model's invented sound.

### Environment

- ⚠ **There is no ffmpeg in the Claude Code container by default.** `npm i ffmpeg-static`
  supplies a static build (7.0.2) — npm is reachable where most hosts are not.
- ⚠ **The cloudfront asset prefix is blocked by the container's egress proxy** (403 on
  CONNECT). Generated clips cannot be downloaded here; they must either be fetched inside
  the Higgsfield sandbox or uploaded into the session by hand.
