# Shape — Coach Acquisition Campaign Plan

**Goal:** Drive signups from personal trainers and nutritionists/dietitians onto the Shape marketplace.
**Primary audience:** Independent personal trainers + nutritionists/dietitians.
**Owner:** Chris · **Prepared:** June 1, 2026 · **Run window:** 4 weeks

---

## 1. The opportunity

Shape is a marketplace of vetted human trainers and nutritionists. Clients pay one $5/mo platform fee; coaches set their own rates and get paid directly — no bundles, no seat math, no revenue split on the coaching itself. For a working coach, that's a low-friction way to add recurring online income on top of in-person clients, with the app handling the whole training loop (train → eat → recover → coach) and even ad-free Shape Radio attached to workouts.

The campaign's job is to make that proposition land with coaches who are currently cobbling together DMs, spreadsheets, and a payment app — and convert them into applicants.

## 2. Positioning & core message

**One line:** *Bring your clients. Keep your rates. We run the loop.*

The pitch to a coach is not "get discovered" (that's a marketplace promise we can't guarantee day one). It's **"run your existing coaching business in one place and keep more of what you earn."** Discovery via the marketplace is the upside, not the lead hook.

**Reasons to believe:**
- You set your own rates and get paid directly — Shape doesn't take a cut of your coaching fees.
- The app loads every client's plan the night before — sets, tempo, cues, macros — so you stop rebuilding spreadsheets.
- One weekly number (Shape Score from sleep, stress, training) tells you who to adjust before they stall.
- Vetted marketplace = you're listed alongside real pros, not buried in an open directory.
- Clients already pay the $5/mo platform fee, so onboarding them costs you nothing.

## 3. Audience segments & angle

| Segment | What they feel today | Lead angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal trainers** | Capped by hours on the gym floor; lose clients who move or travel | "Add online income without adding hours. Your in-person clients keep training with you between sessions." |
| **Nutritionists / dietitians** | Meal plans live in PDFs and email; hard to track adherence | "Sell macro plans and meal coaching where the client actually logs — the ledger ticks live as they eat." |

Keep two message tracks. Same campaign, different proof points and subject lines (see the email sequence file).

## 4. Channels

1. **Direct outreach email (primary).** Your existing list, sequenced 1:1 (see `coach-outreach-email-sequence.md`). This is where signups come from.
2. **LinkedIn + Instagram DM follow-up (secondary).** For non-openers after email #2, a short DM referencing the same offer. Coaches live on IG.
3. **Organic social proof (support).** 2–3 posts per week featuring an early coach's payout/results to warm the list before and during outreach.
4. **Founder-led 1:1 (high-value tail).** For replies and high-follower coaches, a personal Calendly link to onboard them by hand.

## 5. Offer / hook to drive signups

A reason to apply *now*, not later. Pick one:
- **Founding Coach status** — first cohort gets a permanent "Founding Coach" badge on their marketplace profile + priority placement during launch.
- **Zero-friction migration** — "We'll import your current clients and build your first 2 program templates for you." Removes the switching cost that kills coach signups.
- **Launch waiver** — any platform-side fee waived for the first 90 days (confirm with finance before promising).

Recommended: **Founding Coach + done-for-you migration.** Status + removing setup friction beats a discount for this audience.

## 6. Funnel & conversion path

```
List  →  Email 1 (hook)  →  Email 2 (proof)  →  Email 3 (offer/urgency)  →  Email 4 (breakup)
                                   ↓ (no open)                ↓ (reply)
                            IG/LinkedIn DM            Founder 1:1 onboarding
                                                              ↓
                                              Apply → Vetting → Profile live
```

Single CTA throughout: **Apply to coach on Shape** → `theshapecommunity.com/newdesign/SignupTrainer.html`

## 7. Four-week calendar

Send days follow the email sequence (Day 1 / 4 / 9 / 16) so this calendar and the sequence stay on one timeline.

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Hook + proof | Publish 2 social-proof posts. Send Email 1 (Day 1) to the full list (segmented PT vs nutrition), then Email 2 (Day 4) to non-converters. |
| **Week 2** | Offer | Send Email 3 (Day 9, Founding Coach urgency) to non-repliers. Founder books 1:1s with all repliers. Start IG/LinkedIn DMs to non-openers. |
| **Week 3** | Close | Send Email 4 (Day 16, breakup) to remaining non-responders. 2 more posts. |
| **Week 4** | Tally | Finish 1:1s; tally the cohort and close the Founding Coach window. |

## 8. Metrics & targets

Track per segment and overall:

| Metric | Why | Starter target |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | Subject-line fit | 45%+ (warm list) |
| Reply rate | Message-market fit | 8–12% |
| Apply / signup rate | The actual goal | 5–8% of list |
| Apply → live profile | Vetting + onboarding friction | 70%+ |
| Cost per signup | If any paid amplification | track for scale decision |

Define "success" before launch: e.g. *X applications and Y live coach profiles in 4 weeks.* Set X/Y against your list size.

## 9. Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] Confirm the apply/signup URL and that vetting can handle the inbound volume.
- [ ] Confirm what offer you can actually promise (fee waiver, migration help) with finance/ops.
- [ ] Segment the list into PT vs nutritionist for the two message tracks.
- [ ] Set up reply routing + a Calendly link for founder 1:1s.
- [ ] Decide the single success metric and where you'll track it.

## 10. Risks & notes

- **Don't over-promise discovery.** Leading with "get new clients" invites disappointment if marketplace demand is early. Lead with "run your business + keep your rates."
- **Vetting is the bottleneck, not signups.** If applications spike, onboarding capacity caps live coaches — staff for it.
- **Verify every product claim** before send; product copy changes. Claims here are drawn from theshapecommunity.com as of June 1, 2026.
