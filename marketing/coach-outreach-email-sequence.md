# Shape — Coach Outreach Email Sequence

**Goal:** Convert trainers and nutritionists on your list into Shape marketplace applicants.
**Structure:** 4 emails over ~3 weeks. Two tracks — **Personal Trainer (PT)** and **Nutritionist (NUT)** — sharing the same arc with swapped proof points.
**CTA (every email):** Apply to coach on Shape — **PT track →** `theshapecommunity.com/newdesign/SignupTrainer.html` · **NUT track →** `theshapecommunity.com/newdesign/SignupNutritionist.html` (send each track its own apply link — the trainer link routes nutritionists to the wrong intake).

**Personalization tokens:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{specialty}}`, `{{city_or_gym}}`, `{{client_count}}` (use only if known — never fake specificity).

## Send notes

- **Verify the offer before sending Email 3.** The Founding Coach badge, priority placement, and done-for-you migration are still being finalized with ops/finance — keep them conditional ("planned") in copy until signed off, and drop any you can't deliver.
- **Confirm the apply URL per track** — PT → `newdesign/SignupTrainer.html`, NUT → `newdesign/SignupNutritionist.html` (links as of June 1, 2026; send each track its own).
- **Only use a token if you have the value.** A wrong `{{city_or_gym}}` kills trust faster than a generic line.
- **Plain-text, single CTA, one link.** Better deliverability and reply rates than a designed template for 1:1 outreach.
- **Non-openers** of Email 1–2 → route to the IG/LinkedIn DM step in the campaign plan instead of more email.
