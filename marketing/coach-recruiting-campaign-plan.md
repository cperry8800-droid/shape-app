# Shape — Coach Recruiting Campaign Plan

**Goal:** Convert independent trainers and nutritionists into vetted, live coaches on the Shape marketplace.
**Primary audience:** Independent personal trainers + nutritionists/dietitians (supply side).
**Stage:** Pre-launch (app "coming soon"). Recruiting supply *now* so there are coaches to serve members at launch.
**Owner:** Chris · **Prepared:** July 1, 2026 · **Run window:** 4–6 weeks

> **Where this sits.** This is the *direct-response* half of the coach effort — the bottom-of-funnel funnel that turns a coach into an applicant. It builds on and consolidates the June 1 assets (`coach-acquisition-campaign-plan.md` + `coach-outreach-email-sequence.md`) and pairs with `coach-marketing-campaign-plan.md`, the top-of-funnel awareness campaign that feeds this one. Run them together: marketing warms the audience, recruiting closes it.

---

## 1. Objective

Supply comes before demand. A two-sided marketplace with members and no coaches is a dead end, so the single job this run is: **get a first cohort of vetted coaches live before the app ships.** Everything here optimizes for one event — a coach submitting an application — and one downstream event — that application becoming a live, bookable profile.

Success is defined before launch as a concrete pair: **X applications → Y live coach profiles in the run window.** Set X/Y against your actual list size and vetting capacity (see §8).

## 2. Audience & segments

| Segment | What they feel today | Lead angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal trainers** | Capped by hours on the gym floor; lose clients who travel or move | "Add online income without adding hours. Your in-person clients keep training with you between sessions." |
| **Nutritionists / dietitians** | Plans live in PDFs and email; adherence is invisible | "Sell macro plans where the client actually logs — the ledger ticks live as they eat." |
| **Gyms / studios** *(secondary, optional)* | Want a retention tool + online arm for their trainers | "Put your floor staff online under one roof; keep your clients between visits." Only run if intake supports gym/studio listings. |

Two message tracks (PT / NUT), same arc, swapped proof points. Keep them separate all the way to the apply link — the trainer link routes a nutritionist to the wrong intake.

## 3. Positioning & core message

**One line:** *Bring your clients. Keep your rates. We run the loop.*

The lead is **not** "get discovered." Marketplace discovery is real upside but can't be promised while demand is early — leading with it invites disappointment. Lead with **"run your existing coaching business in one place and pay only when you earn."**

**Reasons to believe (verify each against the live site before send):**
- You set your own rates and keep 85% — Shape's commission is a **flat 15%**, charged only when a client pays you (no monthly software fee).
- The app loads every client's plan the night before — sets, tempo, cues, macros — so you stop rebuilding spreadsheets.
- One weekly number (**Shape Score**, from sleep, stress, training) tells you who to adjust before they stall.
- **Vetted** marketplace — you're listed alongside real pros, not buried in an open directory.
- Members pay their own **$5/mo**, and there's no software or setup fee to bring your roster over — Shape earns its 15% only on what you actually get paid.
- Ad-free **Shape Radio** rides along with every workout — a nice-to-have, not the hook.

## 4. Offer / reason to apply now

A reason to act this window, not "someday." Recommended stack:

- **Founding Coach status** — first cohort gets a permanent "Founding Coach" badge + priority placement at launch. *(Planned founding-cohort perk — keep conditional in copy until ops/finance sign off.)*
- **Done-for-you migration** — "We'll import your current clients and build your first two program/meal templates." Removes the switching cost that kills coach signups.
- *(Optional)* **Launch fee waiver** — any platform-side coach fee waived for the first 90 days. Only promise if finance confirms.

Recommended: **Founding Coach + done-for-you migration.** Status + zero setup friction beats a discount for this audience.

## 5. Channels & funnel

Ranked by expected yield for a pre-launch, warm-list run:

1. **Direct outreach email (primary).** Your list, sequenced 1:1 per `coach-outreach-email-sequence.md` (Day 1 / 4 / 9 / 16). This is where applications come from. Plain-text, single CTA, one link, per-track apply URL.
2. **Referral loop (NEW — high yield, low cost).** Every reply and every early applicant gets one ask: "Know one other coach who'd want in on the founding cohort?" Coaches trust coaches; a warm intro out-converts any cold channel. Give them a one-line forwardable blurb + the apply link.
3. **IG / LinkedIn DM follow-up (secondary).** For email non-openers after #2, a short DM referencing the same offer. Coaches live on IG; this recovers the unreachable-by-email tail.
4. **Community seeding (support).** Post the founding-cohort call into the coach communities you're already in (relevant FB groups, Discords, local trainer networks) where self-promo is allowed. Founder voice, not an ad.
5. **Founder-led 1:1 (high-value tail).** Every reply and every high-follower coach gets a personal Calendly link and a hand-onboard. This is the highest-conversion step — protect founder time for it.
6. **Light paid retargeting (optional, pre-launch).** If budget allows, retarget landing-page visitors (coach pages) with a single "Founding Coach — apply" creative. Keep it a closer for warm traffic, not a cold-acquisition spend.

**Single CTA throughout: _Apply to coach on Shape_**
- PT track → `theshapecommunity.com/newdesign/SignupTrainer.html`
- NUT track → `theshapecommunity.com/newdesign/SignupNutritionist.html`


## 6. Calendar (synced to the email sequence)

Send days follow the sequence (Day 1 / 4 / 9 / 16) so the plan and the emails stay on one timeline. Coordinate Week 1 with the awareness campaign's launch so outreach lands on a slightly warmed list.

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Hook + proof | Marketing campaign goes live (social proof warming). Send Email 1 (Day 1) to the full segmented list; Email 2 (Day 4) to non-converters. |
| **Week 2** | Offer | Email 3 (Day 9, Founding Coach urgency) to non-repliers. Founder books 1:1s with all repliers. Start IG/LinkedIn DMs to non-openers. Open the referral ask to early applicants. |
| **Week 3** | Close | Email 4 (Day 16, breakup) to remaining non-responders. Run referral intros. Retargeting closer live if using paid. |
| **Week 4** | Tally + extend | Finish 1:1s; tally the cohort. If under target, run a short "final founding spots" referral/DM push before closing the window. |
| **Weeks 5–6** *(buffer)* | Onboarding | Clear the vetting/onboarding queue so applications become *live profiles* (the real goal), not a backlog. |

## 7. Metrics & targets

Track per segment (PT / NUT) and overall:

| Metric | Why | Starter target |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | Subject-line fit | 45%+ (warm list) |
| Reply rate | Message–market fit | 8–12% |
| Apply / signup rate | The actual goal | 5–8% of list |
| Apply → live profile | Vetting + onboarding friction | 70%+ |
| Referrals generated | Loop health (NEW) | ≥1 referral per 5 applicants |
| Cost per live coach | Only if paid amplification runs | Track for the scale decision |

The number that matters at launch is **live, bookable profiles** — not raw applications. Watch Apply → live profile hardest.

## 8. Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] Confirm the two apply/intake URLs work and route by track (PT vs NUT).
- [ ] Confirm vetting + onboarding can absorb the inbound volume (this, not signups, is the bottleneck).
- [ ] Confirm what you can actually promise (Founding Coach perks, migration help, any fee waiver) with ops/finance — drop anything unconfirmed from copy.
- [ ] Segment the list into PT vs NUT tracks.
- [ ] Set up reply routing + a founder Calendly link for 1:1s.
- [ ] Write the one-line forwardable referral blurb + link.
- [ ] Decide the single success metric (X apps / Y live profiles) and where you'll track it.

## 9. Risks & notes

- **Don't over-promise discovery.** Lead with "run your business + keep your rates," not "get new clients," until marketplace demand is real. (This is exactly why members are Phase 2.)
- **Vetting is the bottleneck, not signups.** If applications spike, onboarding capacity caps live coaches — staff for it before you scale sends.
- **Verify every product claim before send.** Product copy changes; claims here are drawn from the repo/live site as of July 1, 2026. The "verify-then-send" note in the email sequence still applies.
- **Keep Founding Coach perks conditional** in copy until signed off — a promised perk you can't deliver costs you the exact trust this audience runs on.
