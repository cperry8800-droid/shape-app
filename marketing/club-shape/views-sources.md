# Club Shape's filmed views: sources

The stills in `public/club/` (fifteen views, four cuts each), made 2026-11-16 in Runway on the
owner's workspace with `nano-banana-2.1` at 2K, 6 credits an image. `clubViews.mjs` names the
views; `tests/booth-clubViews.test.mjs` holds each file to its size.

Why they exist: owner, 2026-11-16, of the live 3D club beside Nora's portrait and the venue
picture: "make it look like the pictures, more real". Then, watching the first results:
"create some VIP table service on the GA dance floor as well", "as well as the booths and
private rooms", "make a cool DJ booth", then of the sculptural booth that came back, "thats
too small", "and looks like a prop stage", "keep same look as before just maybe a little
bigger booth", "with tables on the sides", "still need the tall ceilings with openings and
views of the city skyline".

How they were made, so a re-run starts from the record:

1. Frames of the real 3D club were rendered from `public/newdesign/booth/noraBooth.mjs` in
   headless Chromium (high quality, the cinematic chain off, 1600x900): the `wide`, `shoulder`
   and `atrium` cameras. They are the layout references, so the wall of LED dots, the balcony
   tiers with palms, the skylight ring and the skyline are the club's own.
2. The Club Shape logo (`public/club-shape-logo-white.svg`) was composited onto the `wide`
   frame's wall before upload, so the generated screen carries the real mark and lettering.
3. Nora's look comes from `public/nora-avatar.png` through one master portrait (task
   `ba862d5e`), then that master is the `@nora` reference everywhere.
4. A layout frame full of 3D figures comes back as 3D figures: the first behind-the-decks try
   (task `ad9c5ab5`, from the raw `shoulder` frame) kept the game-engine crowd and was dropped.
   What worked was a second pass that replaces the people ("Edit @shot ... Replace only the
   foreground crowd with real concert-goers"), and using an already-photoreal still as the
   `@club` reference for the next view.
5. Each tall cut is a recomposition of its wide still ("Recompose @shot as a vertical
   photograph of exactly the same scene"), so the two cuts are one scene.

Encoding: `scripts/club-shape-views/encode.py` (Pillow, Lanczos, cover and centre crop, WebP
method 6; quality 78 for the full cuts and 74 for the small ones, stepping down only where a cut
would pass its cap: the aerial of the grounds is the one that did). Cuts: `-l` 1920x1080,
`-l-sm` 1280x720, `-p` 1080x1920, `-p-sm` 720x1280. ImageMagick's `-quality` is ignored for WebP
in the cloud container, which is why the script is Pillow. The 2K PNGs are not in the repository;
each is recoverable from its task id in Runway.
