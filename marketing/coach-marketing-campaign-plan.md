# Shape — Coach Marketing Campaign Plan (Awareness / Top-of-Funnel)

**Goal:** Build and warm an audience of independent coaches so the recruiting funnel has more, better-qualified applicants to convert.
**Primary audience:** Independent personal trainers + nutritionists/dietitians (supply side).
**Stage:** Pre-launch. Soft CTA is *join the Founding Coach list*, not *apply* — this campaign fills the top of the funnel that `coach-recruiting-campaign-plan.md` closes.
**Owner:** Chris · **Prepared:** July 1, 2026 · **Run window:** 4 weeks (evergreen after)

> **Where this sits.** Recruiting works your warm list 1:1. This campaign builds the audience *beyond* that list — the coaches who've never heard of Shape — and warms them until applying feels obvious. Awareness → opt-in to the Founding Coach list → nurture (`coach-nurture-email-sequence.md`) → apply. Give-first, founder-voiced, not an ad.

---

## 1. Objective

Two jobs, in order:

1. **Grow the list.** Turn strangers into opt-ins on the Founding Coach list. That list is the fuel the recruiting campaign burns — a bigger, warmer list makes every recruiting metric go up.
2. **Establish the coach-side brand.** Before the app ships, plant one idea: *Shape is the platform where you pay only when you earn — a flat 15%, no monthly software fee.* When outreach or a referral lands later, the name is already familiar.

North-star metric: **qualified coaches added to the Founding Coach list.** Everything else (reach, follows) is a means to it.

## 2. Audience & mindset

Coaches scroll IG and (increasingly) TikTok between sessions; the serious-business ones are on LinkedIn. They're skeptical of "software that will change your business" because they've been burned by tools that lock in their clients, bill them monthly whether they work or not, or just add work. They respond to: peers who've figured it out, blunt business math, and give-first coaching value. They tune out: hype, stock-photo gloss, and anything that smells like a hard sell.

## 3. Message platform

Same spine as recruiting, pitched at awareness altitude (educational/aspirational, not "apply now"):

- **Core idea:** *Bring your clients. Keep your rates. We run the loop.*
- **The wedge (lead with this):** most coaching tools bill you a flat monthly fee whether or not you have clients. Shape flips that — you set your own rate and pay a flat 15% *only when a client pays you*, so you owe nothing between clients. (Members pay their own $5/mo.)
- **The relief:** the app runs the train → eat → recover → coach loop, loads each plan the night before, and gives you one weekly number (Shape Score) so you coach instead of doing admin.
- **The status:** vetted marketplace, real pros only. Founding cohort now.

Keep discovery ("get new clients") as *upside*, never the promise — same discipline as recruiting.

## 4. Content pillars

Rotate these; roughly 40% business, 30% product, 20% proof, 10% founder.

| Pillar | Job | Example hooks (house voice) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The business of coaching** | Agitate the real pain (fragmented tools, hours cap, client churn) | "You don't have a client problem — you have a 'your business lives in six apps' problem." · "The math no trainer wants to do: how many hours until you're full?" · "Your clients don't quit because you're bad. They quit because they traveled." |
| **2. How the loop works** | Product education, low-key | "What if the plan loaded the night before — every set, tempo and cue — and you never rebuilt a spreadsheet again?" · "One number that tells you which client is about to stall." |
| **3. Coach proof / spotlights** | Peer credibility (run once founding coaches exist) | "How [Coach] runs 20 online clients without a single PDF." · "[Coach] kept her rates, dropped three apps, and coaches off one screen." |
| **4. Founder POV** | Why Shape exists; trust | "We built Shape so coaches pay only when they earn — a flat 15%, not a monthly fee whether or not you have clients." · "What a fair deal for coaches actually looks like." |
| **5. Give-first coaching value** | Authority without a pitch | Short, genuinely useful coaching/nutrition tips. No CTA — pure value so the account is worth following. |

## 6. Lead magnet + opt-in → handoff

Awareness traffic needs a *soft* next step, not "apply." Capture the opt-in, then let the nurture sequence do the closing.

- **The give (pick one):**
  - **Founding Coach list** — "First cohort gets a permanent badge + priority placement at launch. Get on the list." *(conditional perk — keep framing honest)*
  - **A real resource** — e.g., "The one-page client-progress check we built into Shape Score" or "Program-template starter pack." A give that previews the product beats a generic lead magnet.
- **Opt-in path:** link-in-bio → simple email capture (the GetApp "notify me" pattern already exists for members; mirror it for coaches) → tagged as PT or NUT → enters `coach-nurture-email-sequence.md`.
- **Soft CTA language:** "Get on the Founding Coach list," "Save your spot," "Be first when we open the cohort" — never "apply now" at this stage.

## 7. Four-week content calendar

Cadence: **IG 4×/week, LinkedIn 2×/week, TikTok repurpose 3×/week.** One founder post/week minimum. Week 1 launches a few days *before* the recruiting Email 1 so outreach hits a slightly warmed audience.

| Week | Theme | Posts (example) |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1 — Problem** | Name the pain | Reel (P1): "six apps" hook · Carousel (P1): the hours-cap math · Founder (P4): why we built Shape · Tip (P5) |
| **Week 2 — Mechanism** | Show the relief | Reel (P2): plan loads night-before · Carousel (P2): what Shape Score is · LinkedIn (P1): "keep your rates" business case · Tip (P5) |
| **Week 3 — Proof** | Make it real | Spotlight (P3) if a founding coach exists, else founder demo (P2) · Reel (P1): client-churn angle · Carousel (P4): the "pay only when you earn — flat 15%, no monthly fee" stance · Tip (P5) |
| **Week 4 — Invitation** | Open the loop | Reel (P3/P4): founding cohort is filling · Carousel: "what Founding Coach gets you" (conditional) · LinkedIn (P4): fair-deal-for-coaches manifesto · Direct: "list closes soon" |

Every post ends with the soft CTA to the Founding Coach list except pure give-first tips (which build follow-worthiness).

## 8. Metrics & targets

| Metric | Why | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Founding Coach list opt-ins** | The north star | Set a target vs. list-growth goal; this is what recruiting converts |
| Reach / views by pillar | Which message lands | Double down on the top pillar |
| Saves + shares | Real resonance (beats likes) | Coaches save business math + tips |
| Profile visits → link clicks | Funnel to opt-in | Fix the bio/landing if clicks are high but opt-ins low |
| Follower growth (coach-qualified) | Durable audience | Quality over vanity count |
| Cost per opt-in *(if boosting)* | Scale decision | Only boost proven organic winners |

## 9. Production & repurposing

- **Batch by pillar.** Shoot a week of Reels in one session; write carousels in one sitting. Consistency beats polish.
- **One idea → many cuts.** Each strong hook becomes a Reel, a TikTok, a LinkedIn text post, and a carousel.
- **Founder-voiced, rough is fine.** This audience trusts a real person over a produced brand. Talking-to-camera > motion graphics.
- **Repurpose the proof.** Coach spotlights (P3) are your best asset the moment a founding coach is live — plan to capture their story during onboarding.

## 10. Risks & notes

- **Give-first, not salesy.** If every post is a pitch, the account dies. 4:1 value-to-ask, minimum.
- **Don't over-promise discovery or perks.** Same rule as recruiting: marketplace demand is upside, not a guarantee; Founding Coach perks stay conditional until ops/finance sign off.
- **Verify claims before posting.** The 15% commission, the $5/mo member fee, rates, and Shape Score — all drawn from the repo/live site as of July 1, 2026; re-check before scheduling.
- **Proof needs a coach.** Pillar 3 can't run until a founding coach is live — sequence it after the first recruiting wins, and capture stories during onboarding.
