# Shape — social brand-awareness plan (TikTok + Instagram, Radio-first)

Status: DRAFT brainstorm for the owner · 2026-08-31. Consumer-facing (the coach
funnel lives in the other five files in this folder). Companion evidence:
`docs/MARKET-RESEARCH-2026-07.md`.

---

## The one-line strategy

Paid acquisition is mathematically forbidden for Shape ($4.30–5.50 CPI against a
$5/mo price — the market-research doc's own math), so **organic short-video IS
the channel**. The move that makes it work:

> **Don't market the app's radio feature. Run the brand AS the radio station.**

Every post is a broadcast segment. Radio hands Shape what brand accounts never
have: a **daily reason to post** (stations broadcast every day), **a hundred
years of stealable formats** (morning show · request line · dedications · guest
DJ · countdowns · traffic report · sign-off), **a host character** (Nora, who
already exists), **a set** (a mic + a red ON AIR lamp — ~$25), and an **audio
identity that is legally ours**.

## Why radio-as-channel solves Shape's exact problems

- **The cold start.** There are ~no members yet, so community-UGC content would
  be faked — and the honest-data doctrine extends to marketing. A community
  feed with nobody in it looks dead; **a station with no listeners still sounds
  alive.** Broadcasting is a one-to-many format that needs zero members to work.
- **Character beats logo.** TikTok rewards faces and characters, not brand
  accounts. Nora — the android DJ — already has avatar renders and a TTS voice
  (sage, fitness-instructor style) shipped in the app. Use the product's own
  voice as the channel's voice.
- **The look is already distinctive.** The broadsheet/wire aesthetic
  (Vol. 1 · No. 1 masthead, teal-on-ink, STOP-separated telegram lines) reads
  NOTHING like gym-bro content. Recognizable in half a second of scroll — that
  is what "brand awareness" literally is.
- **Sonic branding.** A 3–5 second original station ident ("You're on Shape
  Radio.") closes every video. Memory compounds; the sound is original audio we
  own, and other creators can reuse it — which is free distribution.

## ⚠ The licensing guardrail — read before filming anything

The app already encodes the Peloton lesson (never stream commercial music we
don't license; link out to Spotify/Apple). **The same rule governs marketing
video:** brand/business accounts get the restricted commercial-music library on
TikTok and Instagram, and commercial tracks can never ride in anything promoted.
So the station's on-air sound is **Nora's voice + original idents + talk**, and
actual playlists are always **link-outs** to Spotify/Apple — exactly the app's
architecture. A music-brand whose videos get muted for licensing would be a
self-inflicted wound; this rule keeps it structurally impossible.

## Pillars & segments — the format library

### 1 · ON AIR — the station (~50% of output, the flagship)

- **The Morning Dispatch** — daily, 15–30s. The red lamp flicks on: *"You're on
  Shape Radio. Today's dispatch: one move — protect the 5:45 session."* The
  app's own daily-directive concept, read as a radio bulletin. Kinetic
  telegram type (STOP separators) over the voice.
- **Now Playing: your heart rate** — the BPM/HR-sync demo. Strap on, music
  locks to the pulse, zones climb. The single most demoable, most novel Radio
  feature — this is the hook video.
- **The Request Line** — 2×/week. *"What's the song that gets you under the
  bar?"* Feature the best answers, credit handles on air. An engagement loop
  that needs zero members to start and turns commenters into regulars.
- **Guest DJ · Coach Residency** — weekly. A founding coach takes the decks:
  their training playlist (link-out), one coaching cue per track, their face on
  the station. They repost it; their audience meets Shape.
- **Shape Sets countdown** — *"Residents who train. Sets that land."* Club
  Shape hype: The Booth hologram, the schedule, behind-the-scenes of building
  the venue. Framed honestly pre-stream: "first broadcast lands when we do."
- **The Training Report** — traffic-report parody. *"Heavy congestion on the
  squat rack from 6 to 7. Take the alternate route: the 5:45 session."*
- **Dedications** — weekly. *"This one goes out to everyone who kept their word
  today."* Ties straight to the app's kept-promise language.
- **The Sign-off** — nightly, 10s. *"That's the broadcast. Log it. — Shape
  Radio."* Consistency is the memory mechanism.

### 2 · The Founder's Wire (~20%)

Build-in-public, weekly: *"I left a decade in finance to build a $5 fitness
app — week N."* What shipped, what broke, real numbers when they exist. The
broadsheet visual grammar makes even a changelog watchable. This is also the
only pillar that can honestly discuss traction before there is any.

### 3 · Product moments (~20%) — ranked by demo-ability

1. **HR → BPM sync** (novel + visual + Radio — the flagship demo)
2. **The share card** — the 1080×1920 story PNG with a real GPS route already
   ships. Market the card itself: *"your run, as a poster."* Every member who
   uses it becomes an ad (the Strava pattern, already built).
3. **Live boosts** — *"Your friend is mid-set RIGHT NOW. Your boost lands
   mid-workout, not after."* The emotional demo.
4. **Cook Mode orchestration** — *"The app runs your kitchen like a head chef:
   while the chicken roasts, start tomorrow's rice."*
5. **Shape Score / never-shaming** — the opinion piece: *"Streaks are a scam.
   Missing a day shouldn't erase your year."* Engineered for debate-stitches.
6. **The Cycle** — the differentiator nobody builds; frame around *discretion*
   (the app never says the word outside its own surfaces) — that restraint IS
   the selling point. Never medical, never prescriptive.
7. **Coach marketplace** — *"$5/month. Coaches price themselves. Coach
   optional."* + the 30-second marathon-plan builder demo.
8. **The telegram cold-open** — pure UI beauty for the design-adjacent
   audience, who share beautiful software unprompted.

### 4 · The Front Page (~10%)

IG carousels + stories in the broadsheet grammar: a daily/weekly **"edition"**
(Vol. 1 · No. N) — one directive, one recipe from Shape Kitchen, one track, one
member (later). Hot-take typography cards. The grid becomes the newspaper.

## Platform playbooks

**TikTok (primary).** 4–7 posts/week. Raw beats polished — phone-shot, hard
cuts, captions on. Hook inside 1.5s or it's dead. SEO the captions ("gym
playlist", "running app", "workout music", "online coach") — TikTok is a search
engine now. 1–3 hashtags, not 30. Reply to good comments WITH video (free
content, compounding reach). Stitch/duet fitness debates with the never-shaming
take. Series names in-frame so formats are followable: ON AIR · The Request
Line · The Founder's Wire.

**Instagram.** Reels = crossposted TikToks (watermark stripped). Carousels are
back — the Front Page editions and Shape Kitchen recipe cards. Stories = song
battles (poll sticker: A vs B — winner "enters rotation"), countdowns to Shape
Sets, this-or-that. **Broadcast Channel named "The Wire"** — the daily dispatch
in text form, perfectly on-brand. The grid IS the broadsheet: alternate
editions with portraits so the profile reads like a masthead.

**YouTube Shorts.** Free redistribution of the same clips. Zero extra effort;
claims the search surface.

## The flywheels (mechanics, not just content)

- **The share card** — already shipped. Every route/PR/plate a member shares to
  Stories is a branded 1080×1920 ad. Make videos teaching the flex.
- **The Request Line credit loop** — commenters get named on air → they return,
  they share the clip they're in.
- **Coach residencies** — each residency is content for us, content for the
  coach, and a recruiting ad (0% BYO) simultaneously.
- **The ident as a public sound** — publish the station ident as an original
  audio others can use; every borrow is distribution.

## 30-day runway

- **Week 1** — build the kit (mic, red lamp, tripod, CapCut, app demo mode on a
  phone frame; record Nora's lines with the app's own TTS). Film the 12 videos
  above in two batch sessions. Post 1/day on TikTok, crosspost Reels.
- **Week 2** — read the data: 3-second hold >65%, completion >45% on shorts,
  **saves + shares as the north star** (ignore follower count for 60 days).
  Kill the weakest format. Air Guest DJ #1.
- **Weeks 3–4** — double the winning format. Launch the Shape Sets countdown.
  Book 3 coach residencies. Make the nightly sign-off habitual. Start the
  Broadcast Channel once Stories have any traction.

## What we deliberately do NOT do

- **No paid ads** — the unit economics forbid it; organic or nothing.
- **No faked community** — no invented testimonials, no demo data presented as
  live members. The honest-data doctrine is a brand asset; keep it on camera.
- **No commercial music baked into brand video** — idents + voice + link-outs,
  per the guardrail above.
- **No follower-count worship** — saves, shares, completion, and profile-tap →
  GetApp clicks are the numbers that matter for the first 60 days.
